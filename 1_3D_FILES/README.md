## Materials

| Part | Material | Why |
|---|---|---|
| All parts | Matte white PLA | rigid enough, and easy to source anywhere |
| Piston | [TPU 95A HF](https://eu.store.bambulab.com/fr/products/tpu-95a-hf) | flexible, so it seals against the syringe wall |

The piston is the only part that is not printed rigid. A rigid piston does not seal: the
paste creeps back up along the barrel wall instead of coming out of the tip. Printed in TPU,
the piston deforms slightly against the glass and acts as its own gasket.

TPU prints slowly and does not tolerate retraction well. Print it with no retraction, at low
speed, and with a direct drive extruder if you have one.
